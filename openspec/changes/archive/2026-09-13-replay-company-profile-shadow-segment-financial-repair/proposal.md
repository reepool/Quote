## Why

The immutable twenty-report routing/continuation replay completed all reports with
source-traceable facts, but every report remained `hold`. Source-bound review showed
that segment-financial completion was the largest blocker, and the archived
provider-free repair now needs one empirical replay to determine whether the same
frozen cohort becomes broadly usable without weakening semantic safeguards.

## What Changes

- Freeze the existing twenty-report manifest, PDFs, v5 Evidence plan and audits, prior
  routing/continuation replay, archived segment-financial closure audit, current
  segment implementation hashes, Gemini profile, budgets, review rule, and readiness
  gates as one replay contract.
- Add one narrow replay identity and operator mode that delegates to the existing
  report-local shadow service and fails before provider creation on any contract drift
  or pre-existing output.
- Run the frozen cohort exactly once under a new immutable batch ID, preserving every
  report-local success and typed failure without prompt/schema/model/parameter tuning,
  targeted reruns, or result splicing.
- Publish a complete source review, readiness audit, segment-blocker recurrence audit,
  and immutable comparison against the routing/continuation baseline.
- Close as `ready`, `hold`, or `failed` from the frozen empirical gates while keeping
  every artifact research-only and `production_authorization=not_authorized`.
