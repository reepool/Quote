## Why

The September 11 Evidence-role shadow replay completed all twenty reports, but
seventeen operating-quantity scopes ended in `candidate_schema_invalid` and accounted
for fifty-four `required_coverage_missing` findings. The failures were not caused by
transport or output truncation: bounded context pages and broad selector signals still
expanded request fields beyond the fields directly owned by the source page, so valid
quantity disclosures were lost when the model also returned unsupported legal-empty
coverage.

## What Changes

- Derive operating-quantity scope fields from field-specific signals on direct candidate
  pages; adjacent context and broad section/reason labels must not add numeric fields.
- Preserve field-level Evidence ownership in prepared shadow scopes and make the
  existing Stage 5 request builder respect explicit bindings instead of recreating a
  page-by-field cross product.
- Keep contextual pages available for interpretation while preventing them from
  supporting candidates or legal-empty coverage for unowned fields.
- Rebuild and compare the frozen twenty-report plan provider-free, covering all seventeen
  affected operating scopes and retaining positive capacity/quantity disclosures.
- Record the observed token distribution and keep the existing output budgets unchanged
  in this change because every over-budget response succeeded and none of the thirty-five
  failed traces was a truncation failure.
- Keep all artifacts research-only with `production_authorization=not_authorized`; do not
  run an LLM, mutate the authoritative batch, or open Stage 6 or production consumers.

## Impact

- Affects the existing shadow Evidence planner/preparer, the Stage 5 request Evidence

- Does not change public APIs, semantic object types, Gold expectations, LLM model,
  timeout/output budgets, historical report bundles, approved storage, scheduler or
  backfill paths, commodity exposure, value-chain publication, DCF, or Stage 6.
