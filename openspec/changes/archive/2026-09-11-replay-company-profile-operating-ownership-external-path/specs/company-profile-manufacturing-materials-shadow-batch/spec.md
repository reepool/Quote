## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: External-path ownership recovery replay is single-use and hash-bound

The system MUST admit exactly one transport-recovery replay of the frozen twenty-report
manufacturing/materials cohort under
`manufacturing-materials-shadow-operating-ownership-external-gemini-20260911-a`.
Admission MUST validate the archived failed ownership replay and its typed transport
evidence, cohort/PDF identities, ownership-aware v6 plan and preparation, ownership audits,
current implementation, Gemini route, fixed provider budgets, review rule, readiness gates,
new batch identity, and output absence before provider construction.

#### Scenario: Exact transport-recovery contract is admitted

- **WHEN** every frozen artifact, hash, provider parameter, failed-attempt input, batch ID,
  and output-absence condition matches
- **THEN** the existing report-local service may execute all twenty reports once under the
  external recovery identity
- **AND** neither the failed sandbox batch nor an earlier authoritative batch is modified.

#### Scenario: Recovery input or identity drifts

- **WHEN** a cohort, PDF, plan, preparation, ownership audit, failed-attempt artifact,
  implementation, route, budget, batch ID, or output condition differs
- **THEN** admission fails before provider construction with the exact drift class
- **AND** the rejection does not authorize another identity or relaxed contract.

### Requirement: The recovery replay starts through the authorized external path

The read-only Scorpio/Gemini preflight and the provider-bearing recovery replay MUST start
through the user's authorized external path to `scorpio.reepool.com`. After the first
semantic request begins, the batch MUST be the sole authoritative transport-recovery replay
and MUST close as `ready`, `hold`, or `failed` without sandbox fallback, targeted rerun,
model substitution, Evidence change, token tuning, or candidate splicing.

#### Scenario: External preflight succeeds

- **WHEN** the frozen route resolves, connects, and returns the expected logical model
  profile through the external path
- **THEN** the same authorized path may launch the sole complete replay
- **AND** the preflight creates no semantic candidates.

#### Scenario: A request fails after the replay begins

- **WHEN** a report or scope ends with a typed transport, deadline, truncation, parse,
  schema, verification, Evidence, or contract failure
- **THEN** the report-local result is preserved and execution follows the existing
  isolation policy
- **AND** no second recovery batch or imported candidate is permitted.

### Requirement: External recovery results are reviewed and compared immutably

The system MUST generate the frozen source-bound review package, empirical readiness audit,
seventeen-scope ownership recurrence audit, and immutable comparison outside all input
batch directories. The comparison MUST bind every input by content hash and report
execution completion, report usability, accepted facts, Evidence traceability, affected
scope outcomes, provider calls and typed failures, token usage, latency, unresolved-review
median/p90, sampled precision, critical semantic errors, and readiness decision. Missing or
unreviewed required evidence MUST prevent a favorable result.

#### Scenario: Complete external recovery batch is reviewed

- **WHEN** the batch and source review are complete and every required input hash validates
- **THEN** the audits record every metric, affected scope, source-bound finding, failed gate,
  and before/after delta without modifying a batch
- **AND** the validation is complete whether the decision is `ready`, `hold`, or `failed`.

#### Scenario: Review or comparison input is incomplete

- **WHEN** a manifest, report, review row, readiness input, or required hash is missing or
  invalid
- **THEN** readiness remains `hold` or `failed` with the exact missing input
- **AND** no summary reconstruction or additional provider execution fills the gap.

### Requirement: External recovery remains research-only under fixed budgets

The recovery replay MUST use Gemini, extract/verify output limits `20000/18000`, a
300-second request timeout, no outer deadline, and a 600-physical-call ceiling. Every proof,
trace, report, review, readiness result, and comparison MUST retain
`production_authorization=not_authorized`. Accepted facts MUST remain restricted research
records and MUST NOT enter approved storage, Stage 6, scheduler/backfill, commodity
exposure, value-chain publication, DCF, or another production consumer.

#### Scenario: Large valid response or typed truncation is observed

- **WHEN** a request reports output-budget excess or a typed truncation result
- **THEN** the actual usage and classification are retained under the frozen budget
- **AND** the request is not split or rerun with a different output limit.

#### Scenario: All empirical readiness gates pass

- **WHEN** execution completion is at least 95%, at least 90% of reports are usable or
  usable-with-caveats, Evidence traceability is 100%, source review is complete with at
  least 99% sampled precision and zero critical semantic errors, and unresolved-review
  median/p90 are no greater than two/five
- **THEN** the result may recommend only a separate restricted promotion proposal
- **AND** no production authorization or downstream write is granted by this change.
