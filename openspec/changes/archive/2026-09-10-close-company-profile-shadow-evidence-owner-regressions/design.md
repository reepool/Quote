## Context

The immutable external precision replay is `hold`: 19 reports are `hold`, one is `failed`, usable rate is zero, and review workload remains high. Its first review classified eight chapter samples as noncritical errors, but direct recheck shows `920033.BJ` is a valid owner-bound legal empty because the source page explicitly states `主要原材料及能源情况 □适用 √不适用`. The remaining seven errors are six owner-routing defects and one overview-substance defect.

The existing `shadow_evidence` planner, Stage 5 normalizer/service, review models, and provider-free audit patterns remain authoritative. This change does not create another execution or validation framework.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Correct the reviewed precision baseline additively and hash-bind the correction to the archived v1 package/outcomes/readiness/comparison.
- Prevent legal-empty acceptance from non-owning operating-quantity, material, and segment pages.
- Preserve positive issuer capacity Evidence when a governed overview passage explicitly discloses capacity.
- Reject cross-reference-only BusinessOverview content while retaining substantive issuer business descriptions.
- Prove the seven confirmed rows closed provider-free and record zero provider calls.

**Non-Goals:**

- No LLM run, Evidence-plan replay, new field/schema, prompt, model, token/deadline change, generalized retrieval system, or production promotion.
- No mutation of the external batch, archived v1 audit, historical four-report/OOS results, or Gold.
- No Stage 6, approved writer, scheduler/backfill, commodity exposure, value-chain publication, or DCF.

## Decisions

1. **Correct the audit additively.** Keep the archived v1 artifacts immutable and write a v2 correction/outcome/readiness chain that changes only the `920033.BJ` classification from `noncritical_error` to `correct`. This makes the corrected baseline 76/83 (91.57%) with zero critical errors and retains `hold`.

2. **Fix owner selection in the existing planner.** Refine `_chapter_owner_score`, supported-field selection, and candidate-page ownership only where the seven frozen source shapes prove a defect. Do not introduce a new owner registry. Explicit issuer capacity wording remains positive Evidence even when it appears in a principal-business/overview passage; industry capacity and production-planning vocabulary do not establish issuer legal-empty coverage.

3. **Legal empty requires an owning disclosure, not absence in a selected page.** Operating quantity/capacity, material input, and segment dimension may close as `not_disclosed`/`not_applicable` only from their governed owner section or explicit applicability row. Shareholder, industry, generic cost, and adjacent context pages cannot close those fields.

4. **Business overview must be substantive.** A value consisting only of `参见`/`详见`/equivalent cross-reference wording is rejected or left unresolved. The system may still use substantive business text present in the same owner Evidence; it must not accept the pointer itself as the profile statement.

5. **Use frozen rows as regressions.** Provider-free fixtures bind the seven confirmed rows and the corrected `920033.BJ` control. A closure audit records the exact outcome, source hashes, zero provider calls, and `production_authorization=not_authorized`.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **[Owner rules become too strict]** Valid disclosures in unusual sections could become unresolved. → Keep positive text-pattern support and add the explicit capacity-in-overview and explicit material-applicability controls.
- **[Cross-reference rejection loses nearby substance]** The same Evidence may contain useful business text outside the selected pointer. → Reject only the cross-reference target; allow a substantive target bound to the same owner Evidence.
- **[Provider-free closure is mistaken for readiness]** Seven fixtures cannot prove cohort usability. → Keep the external replay `hold`, make zero provider calls, and require a separate later empirical replay proposal.
