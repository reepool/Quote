## Why

The provider-free v5 Evidence plan closes the three source-reviewed routing and
continuation defects, but no semantic result yet proves that those corrections improve
twenty-report usability or review workload. One immutable provider-bearing replay is
needed to measure the corrected plan under the existing production-readiness gates.

## What Changes

- Freeze the committed twenty-report manifest/PDFs, v5 Evidence plan, preparation
  audit, routing/continuation correction audit, and their hashes as replay inputs.
- Add one narrow replay contract and new batch identity using the existing Gemini
  profile, semantic service, output limits, deadline, call ceiling, source-review rule,
  and readiness thresholds.
- Run the twenty reports exactly once through the user-authorized sandbox-external
  network path, preserving every report-local success and typed failure without tuning,
  targeted reruns, or result splicing.
- Publish the complete source review, readiness audit, and immutable comparison against
  the owner-closure replay, including recurrence of the three corrected defects and the
  previously closed high-risk semantic errors.
- Close as `ready`, `hold`, or `failed` from measured results; keep all outputs
  research-only and `production_authorization=not_authorized`.
