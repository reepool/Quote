## Context

The authoritative Evidence-role replay
`manufacturing-materials-shadow-evidence-role-gemini-20260911-a` completed its twenty
reports, but seventeen operating scopes produced `candidate_schema_invalid` after broad
page-by-field Evidence ownership let context pages support unrelated numeric fields and
legal-empty coverage. The archived ownership change introduced plan version
`manufacturing_materials_shadow.2026-09-11.6`, reduced the affected scopes' prepared
bindings from 221 legacy cross-products to 40 explicit owners, retained known positive
disclosures, and proved the correction without a provider.

The existing planner/preparer, Stage 5 semantic owner,
`ManufacturingMaterialsShadowBatchService`, immutable report store, review builder,
readiness builder, and comparison builder remain authoritative. This change measures the
causal correction; it does not create another extraction or evaluation path.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Rebuild and freeze the v6 Evidence plan and preparation audit from the same twenty
  report/PDF inputs before provider construction.
- Bind the manifest, v6 artifacts, ownership audit, prior authoritative batch, current
  implementation, Gemini settings, readiness gates, and absent output to one proof.
- Execute the complete cohort once under a new immutable identity.
- Produce complete source review, operating-failure recurrence, readiness, and immutable
  before/after comparison artifacts.

**Non-Goals:**

- No cohort/PDF change, prompt/schema or semantic-policy relaxation, dynamic token
  implementation, timeout change, model comparison, Gold change, targeted rerun,
  candidate splice, or historical mutation.
- No new semantic service, generalized admission framework, production writer, Stage 6,
  approved table, scheduler/backfill, commodity exposure, value-chain publication, or
  DCF path.

## Decisions

1. **Use a new v6 plan and preparation, not the old v5 prepared scopes.** Ownership is
   now a planning/preparation contract. Reusing the v5 preparation would omit the fix;
   rewriting the historical batch would destroy the comparison.
2. **Add one narrow replay contract/mode.** The existing operator validates the new
   proof and delegates to the current report-local service. This is smaller and safer
   than another runner or orchestration layer.
3. **Use one immutable batch identity.** The only provider-bearing identity is
   `manufacturing-materials-shadow-operating-ownership-gemini-20260911-a`. After its
   first semantic request, any success or typed failure consumes the experiment.
4. **Freeze provider parameters.** The replay uses
   `semantic_extraction__scorpio_gemini`, extract/verify limits `20000/18000`, a
   300-second request timeout, a 600-physical-call ceiling, and no outer hard deadline.
   This isolates Evidence ownership from token or transport changes.
5. **Treat external permission as transport-only.** A read-only sandbox preflight that
   fails due to DNS/network isolation may be repeated outside the sandbox. The replay
   command itself may use the user's standing Scorpio transfer authorization without
   changing any semantic input or parameter.
6. **A non-favorable result completes the experiment.** `ready`, `hold`, and `failed`
   are honest terminal results. Remaining errors become source-bound follow-up evidence,
   not a reason to rerun or tune this batch.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **[The v6 rebuild does not reproduce its archived audit hash]** → stop before provider
  construction and record the exact manifest, plan, preparation, or implementation
  drift.
- **[Scorpio fails after execution begins]** → preserve typed report-local failures and
  close the single authoritative batch; do not launch a replacement identity.
- **[Ownership improves schema yield but workload gates still fail]** → disclose the
  remaining reason codes and close `hold`; a later change may address one demonstrated
  cause.
- **[Large outputs are mistaken for this causal defect]** → retain fixed output budgets
  and record actual truncation classifications; dynamic tiers require a separate
  controlled experiment.
- **[Research readiness is mistaken for production approval]** → retain
  `production_authorization=not_authorized` in every output and keep all production
  consumers closed.
