## Why

The latest shared Stage 5 fixes remove defect classes that appeared across several
models, but historical replay alone cannot show whether the corrected contract works on
previously unseen annual reports. A small frozen fresh cohort is needed now to measure
generalization before any production-promotion proposal, without returning to
company-by-company repair loops.

## What Changes

- Select and freeze three to four locally valid manufacturing/materials annual reports
  that have not participated in prior company-profile authority, Gold, OOS, canary,
  shadow, targeted, or adjudication runs.
- Freeze a six-core-chapter Evidence plan for every report before provider access,
  including legal-empty and known-limitation decisions without embedding expected
  answers.
- Admit exactly one immutable provider-bearing fresh-cohort batch through the existing
  Stage 5 owner, current `semantic_extraction` four-model pool, and current dynamic token
  policy.
- Measure execution completion, research usability, Evidence traceability, and
  recurrence of the recently fixed overview-boundary, Evidence-owner, and unsupported
  event-name defect families.
- Produce a source-bound review and close as observed. `usable_with_caveats`, `hold`, or
  typed `failed` are honest validation results and do not authorize targeted reruns or
  in-change semantic tuning.
- Retain `production_authorization=not_authorized` and keep Stage 6, approved writes,
  scheduler/backfill, CommodityExposure, ValueChainRole, and DCF closed.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `company-profile-manufacturing-materials-shadow-batch`: admit one small, frozen,
  previously unseen cohort for current-contract generalization validation and require an
  immutable source-bound outcome without post-call tuning or reruns.

## Impact

- Affects the existing company-profile shadow-batch manifest/admission path, preparation
  artifacts, focused tests, and one new research-only validation artifact set.
- Reuses the existing Stage 5 semantic service, provider adapter, four-model logical
  route, dynamic token calculation, projection, report benchmark, and immutable bundle
  storage; no parallel extraction or retry system is introduced.
- Does not modify prior bundles, Gold expectations, production databases, public APIs,
  approved writers, schedules, legacy backfill, or downstream investment outputs.
