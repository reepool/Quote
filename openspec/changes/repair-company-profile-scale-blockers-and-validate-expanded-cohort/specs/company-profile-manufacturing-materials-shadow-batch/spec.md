## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Observed Evidence-owner misses are corrected in the existing selector
The manufacturing/materials Evidence selector MUST recognize the report-local operating
capacity/quantity and business-regime disclosure shapes documented in the September 13 fresh
cohort when those pages contain issuer-owned numeric tables or governed explicit legal-empty
wording. It MUST retain complete owning headers, units, footnotes, continuation pages, and
physical hashes. Industry-only capacity commentary, contextual cross-references, generic
financial notes, and narrower control-scope statements MUST remain non-owning. The correction
MUST use the existing selector and Evidence-plan contract rather than a new planning path.

#### Scenario: Issuer capacity disclosure was missed
- **WHEN** a frozen annual-report page contains an issuer-owned capacity table or statement
  with a supported numeric quantity and unit in one of the reviewed source shapes
- **THEN** the selector includes the owning page range and existing operating-capacity field
  binding
- **AND** adjacent industry commentary alone cannot become the owner.

#### Scenario: Governed legal-empty wording was missed
- **WHEN** a reviewed operating-quantity or business-regime disclosure explicitly supplies
  the existing governed not-applicable result
- **THEN** the selector includes the field-owning Evidence needed by the existing legal-empty
  verifier
- **AND** a context-only or narrower no-change statement remains insufficient.

### Requirement: Common scale-blocker repairs are validated on one expanded frozen cohort
The system MUST freeze exactly eight local-valid manufacturing/materials annual reports whose
identities are absent from prior company-profile authority, Gold, OOS, canary, shadow,
targeted, adjudication, and fresh-cohort inputs. Before provider access it MUST freeze report
and PDF identities plus one complete six-core-chapter Evidence plan for every report. It MUST
then execute exactly one new immutable batch through the existing four-model logical route and
Stage 5 owner. After the first semantic call, prompts, Evidence, fields, route, models, token
policy, deadlines, semantic rules, and acceptance policy MUST remain unchanged; no targeted
rerun or historical splice is permitted.

#### Scenario: Eight unseen reports are prepared
- **WHEN** all eight reports have local-valid PDFs, unique unseen identities, complete chapter
  plans, owner bindings, known limitations, and content hashes
- **THEN** admission records the frozen manifest and preparation audit before provider access
- **AND** no Gold answer or expected runtime fact is present in the inputs.

#### Scenario: The expanded batch runs once
- **WHEN** frozen inputs, route fingerprint, dynamic token policy, output identity, and
  research boundary validate and the first semantic request begins
- **THEN** the existing report-local service executes the batch once with bounded internal
  four-model failover and complete attempt lineage
- **AND** every success, `hold`, or typed `failed` result remains under that identity.

#### Scenario: Sandbox DNS blocks an authorized endpoint
- **WHEN** a preflight or provider request encounters a likely sandbox-related DNS or network
  permission failure for an authorized Scorpio, ZAI, or DeepSeek endpoint
- **THEN** the same operation may use the user-authorized external-sandbox path without
  changing the request contract or batch identity
- **AND** the permission change does not authorize a retry loop or replacement run.

### Requirement: Expanded-cohort audit measures the five repaired families
The system MUST create immutable source-bound review and audit artifacts outside the report
bundles. They MUST bind inputs and outputs by content hash and report execution completion,
status distribution, accepted facts, Evidence traceability, provider/model attempts, token
usage, latency, typed failures, and human-review workload. Review MUST cover every blocker,
caveat, unresolved item, one stable accepted or legal-empty result per core chapter per report,
and recurrence of unfair route exhaustion, cross-period segment-row collapse, satisfied
coverage false negatives, explicit `分部间抵销` rejection, and the reviewed Evidence-owner
misses.

#### Scenario: Source review completes
- **WHEN** the sole batch has ended and every required review row is resolved against frozen
  physical Evidence
- **THEN** the audit reports exact recurrence counts and business impact for all five repaired
  families plus all contracted execution and usability metrics
- **AND** it closes from observed results without post-hoc Gold or another provider run.

#### Scenario: A repaired family recurs
- **WHEN** source review finds one of the five named false-negative or execution families
- **THEN** the exact report, scope, Evidence, route attempt, runtime target, and recommendation
  remain disclosed
- **AND** the change does not tune rules or rerun that case.

### Requirement: Expanded-cohort validation remains research-only
The system SHALL retain `production_authorization=not_authorized` on every expanded-cohort
manifest, plan, request, trace, candidate, disposition, projection, report, review, and audit.
Accepted facts MUST remain `accepted_for_review` research fixtures under current usage
restrictions. This change MUST NOT write approved data or enable Stage 6, scheduler/backfill,
CommodityExposure, ValueChainRole, DCF, or another production publication path.

#### Scenario: Expanded cohort is favorable
- **WHEN** the reviewed cohort shows materially improved execution and research usability
  without a critical semantic error
- **THEN** the audit may recommend a separate restricted production-promotion proposal
- **AND** no production authorization is granted by this change.

#### Scenario: Expanded cohort remains hold or failed
- **WHEN** one or more reports or empirical gates remain `hold` or `failed`
- **THEN** the audit records the exact remaining distance and still completes this validation
- **AND** it does not trigger company-specific reruns or production activation.
