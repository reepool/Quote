## Decisions

1. **A new identity is mandatory.** Use `manufacturing-materials-shadow-segment-normalization-external-live-20260911-b`; the consumed sandbox identity and result remain immutable.
2. **Only execution permission changes.** The two scope identities, report hashes, prepared Evidence, Gemini profile, `20000/18000` output limits, 300-second deadline, and 12-call shared ceiling remain identical.
3. **The existing business owner still executes semantics.** The change-local operator validates all frozen inputs before provider construction and delegates to `ManufacturingMaterialsProfileSliceService.execute_prepared_report`.
4. **Transfer authorization is narrow and recorded.** Only content required by the two frozen semantic requests may be sent to `scorpio.reepool.com`; no unrelated workspace files are uploaded.
5. **Results remain case-local.** A case resolves only with accepted source-bound rows, completed scope coverage, and absence of its targeted normalization failure. A semantic or execution failure is retained without rerun.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **[External Scorpio still fails]** → Persist the typed failure and stop; do not change model or timeout.
- **[Only one case resolves]** → Treat the result as partial empirical evidence and do not authorize a cohort replay automatically.
- **[Both cases resolve]** → The result may justify proposing one later twenty-report replay, but does not itself alter readiness or production status.
