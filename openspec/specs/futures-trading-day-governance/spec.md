## Purpose
Define exchange-scoped futures trading-day governance that converts official calendars, holiday notices, sessions, and reviewed overrides into auditable target dates, while preventing weekday estimates or unresolved dates from silently authorizing market-data downloads.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Futures Trading Day Governance Must Own Exchange Calendars
The system SHALL maintain governed futures exchange calendars in `futures.db` with trading-day status, timezone, session type, source profile, parser version, quality flag, evidence metadata, and update timestamps.

#### Scenario: Exchange calendar row is official
- **WHEN** an official exchange calendar or holiday notice identifies a trading day or non-trading day
- **THEN** the stored row SHALL include the exchange, trade date, trading status, source profile, parser version, quality flag, evidence URL or notice id, and parser metadata

#### Scenario: Exchange calendar row is estimated
- **WHEN** the system creates a weekday-seed fallback calendar row
- **THEN** the row SHALL be marked with `quality_flag=estimated`
- **AND** the row SHALL NOT be treated as official calendar coverage

### Requirement: Futures Trading Day Governance Must Support Instrument Sessions
The system SHALL maintain instrument-level session rules that refine exchange calendars for night sessions, no-night-session products, trade-date roll behavior, timezone, and product-level exceptions.

#### Scenario: Instrument has a night session
- **WHEN** an instrument has a configured night-session rule
- **THEN** the session record SHALL expose the local start/end times, exchange timezone, and trade-date attribution rule

#### Scenario: Instrument has a product-level exception
- **WHEN** a product is listed, delisted, suspended, has no night session, or has a temporary session adjustment
- **THEN** the governance service SHALL apply an instrument-level override without rewriting unrelated exchange-level calendar rows

### Requirement: Official Notices Must Be Auditable
The system SHALL persist official calendar, holiday, trading-hour, and special-closure notice evidence before or while deriving governed calendar rows from that evidence.

#### Scenario: Notice is parsed successfully
- **WHEN** an official notice parser extracts dates, scope, and trading status with sufficient confidence
- **THEN** the system SHALL store the notice evidence and derived calendar changes with parser version and source lineage

#### Scenario: Notice cannot be parsed confidently
- **WHEN** an official notice is reachable but ambiguous, structurally changed, or conflicts with existing calendar rows
- **THEN** the system SHALL create a review-required record rather than silently guessing calendar changes

### Requirement: Manual Calendar Review Must Be Explicit
The system SHALL support explicit manual review decisions for unresolved futures trading-day calendar notices and conflicts.

#### Scenario: Reviewer confirms a calendar decision
- **WHEN** an operator manually confirms a trading-day, non-trading-day, session, or override decision
- **THEN** the stored review record SHALL include reviewer, decision, reason, evidence reference, scope, effective dates, and timestamp

#### Scenario: Manual decision changes production behavior
- **WHEN** a manual review decision is used to permit provider requests or production writes
- **THEN** the ingestion run metadata SHALL include the manual override reference

### Requirement: Target Trading Dates Must Be Generated By Governance
The system SHALL generate target trading-date sets for futures sync, backfill, and dry-run from governed calendars instead of natural calendar iteration.

#### Scenario: Date range contains non-trading days
- **WHEN** a futures sync or backfill receives a natural start date and end date
- **THEN** trading-day governance SHALL return only valid target trading dates for each relevant exchange unless an explicit override includes non-trading dates

#### Scenario: Target date set is generated
- **WHEN** target trading dates are resolved
- **THEN** the result SHALL include exchange, trade dates, source quality summary, skipped non-trading-day count, unresolved conflict count, and any review-required blockers

### Requirement: Calendar Quality Gates Must Protect Production Writes
The system SHALL enforce configurable minimum calendar-quality thresholds before production futures price writes.

#### Scenario: Calendar quality is sufficient
- **WHEN** all target exchanges meet the configured minimum calendar quality
- **THEN** production sync or backfill MAY proceed to provider fetching

#### Scenario: Calendar quality is insufficient
- **WHEN** a target exchange only has estimated or conflicting calendar rows and production writes require verified calendar quality
- **THEN** the system SHALL block production writes and return a clear governance blocker

#### Scenario: Dry-run uses estimated calendar
- **WHEN** an operator runs dry-run with estimated calendar rows
- **THEN** the dry-run MAY proceed
- **AND** the output SHALL visibly report the estimated calendar risk

### Requirement: Governance Readiness Must Be Queryable
The system SHALL expose local read paths for futures trading-day governance status, calendar evidence, review-required notices, and target-date expansion diagnostics.

#### Scenario: Governance status is queried
- **WHEN** a caller queries futures trading-day governance readiness
- **THEN** the response SHALL include exchange coverage, latest governed date, quality distribution, unresolved conflicts, review-required count, and production-write eligibility

#### Scenario: Calendar evidence is queried
- **WHEN** a caller queries a calendar row or date range
- **THEN** the response SHALL include source profile, quality flag, evidence reference, and manual override reference when present

### Requirement: Official holiday notices define full-exchange closures
The trading-day governance service SHALL derive full-exchange closed dates from each configured exchange's own official holiday notice. A shared date list across exchanges SHALL NOT replace per-exchange notice evidence.

#### Scenario: Closure range is parsed
- **WHEN** an official notice for one exchange states an inclusive full-day closure range
- **THEN** the service SHALL store that notice in `futures_calendar_notices`
- **AND** it SHALL write one governed closed calendar row for each date in the range with `quality_flag=backfilled_verified`, `source_profile=exchange_official_holiday_notice`, `classification_rule=official_holiday_notice`, the notice id, and the notice URL
- **AND** a bare month-day SHALL use the notice's stated year, while an explicit other year in the same sentence SHALL be kept

#### Scenario: The same closure is announced by another exchange
- **WHEN** a second exchange publishes the same closure dates in its own notice
- **THEN** the service SHALL store a separate notice and separate calendar rows for that exchange

#### Scenario: Notice text does not yield a confident range
- **WHEN** a fetched holiday notice contains no confident closure range
- **THEN** the service SHALL create a review-required record
- **AND** it SHALL NOT write guessed closed or open rows from that notice

#### Scenario: One stated date disagrees with a verified row
- **WHEN** a notice closes a date already verified as a trading day, or opens a date already verified as closed for a different reason than the weekend rule
- **THEN** the service SHALL create a review-required record for that date and SHALL NOT overwrite it
- **AND** other confident ranges in the same notice SHALL still be written
- **AND** a date the notice closes that is already closed, including a deterministic weekend, SHALL be kept closed and MAY be updated to `official_holiday_notice`

#### Scenario: A later notice revises the year
- **WHEN** a newer official notice for the same exchange states a different status for dates already derived from an earlier notice
- **THEN** the service SHALL apply only the dates that newer notice explicitly states
- **AND** it SHALL NOT drop closures from the earlier notice that the newer notice does not mention

### Requirement: Night-session suspension is not a closed trading day
The governance service SHALL record an official night-session suspension without changing that date's trading-day status.

#### Scenario: Notice suspends only the night session
- **WHEN** an official notice says a named date has no night session and does not include that date in a full-day closure
- **THEN** the service SHALL store the suspension on the notice
- **AND** it SHALL NOT set `is_trading_day=false` for that date
- **AND** when that date already is or later becomes a verified trading day, the calendar row metadata SHALL include the night-session suspension

### Requirement: Official notice overrides the weekend closure rule
A weekend SHALL remain closed only when no official notice explicitly marks that weekend date as a trading day.

#### Scenario: Notice names a weekend as open
- **WHEN** an official holiday notice explicitly marks a Saturday or Sunday as a trading day
- **THEN** the governed calendar row SHALL be a trading day sourced from that notice
- **AND** the deterministic weekend rule SHALL NOT overwrite it as closed

#### Scenario: Notice agrees the weekend is closed
- **WHEN** an official notice only repeats that a weekend is closed, or says nothing about that weekend
- **THEN** the existing weekend closure rule SHALL still close that date

### Requirement: Notice-closed dates are absent from target trading dates
Target-date expansion SHALL exclude dates governed as closed by an official holiday notice.

#### Scenario: Sync window contains a notice holiday
- **WHEN** trading-day governance expands a range that includes a date closed by `official_holiday_notice`
- **THEN** that date SHALL NOT be a target trading date
- **AND** the expansion SHALL count it as a skipped non-trading day
- **AND** it SHALL NOT be an unresolved or review-required blocker
